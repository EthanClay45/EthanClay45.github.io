Privacy Policy

Edu Shorts Uploader · Last updated November 7, 2026

What the app does

Edu Shorts Uploader is a personal tool that uploads videos to its owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is used only by its owner and is not offered to the public.

What data it uses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app receives permission to manage that YouTube channel. It uses this permission only to:

  • read the channel's name and ID, to confirm which channel it is connected to;
  • upload videos and set their title, description, privacy status and publish time;
  • edit the title, description or privacy status of videos it uploaded.

Where data is stored

The Google sign-in token is saved only on the owner's own computer. It is never sent to any server other than Google's. The app has no servers or databases of its own and collects no analytics.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone. Data goes only to Google/YouTube, as needed to upload and manage the owner's videos.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or through Google security settings. Deleting the saved token file from the owner's computer also disconnects it.
