Edu Shorts Uploader

A private tool that uploads educational YouTube Shorts to its owner's own channel.

Edu Shorts Uploader is part of a personal video-making workflow. It produces short "How X becomes Y" explainer videos and uses the YouTube Data API to upload them to the owner's YouTube channel, usually as private videos that the owner reviews and publishes by hand.

It is not a public service: there are no accounts to create, and it only acts on the YouTube channel that its owner signs in with.

Read the privacy policy to see what data the app uses and how to revoke its access.
